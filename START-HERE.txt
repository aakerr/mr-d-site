========================================================================
  MR. D'S CLASSROOM OS  —  START HERE
========================================================================

TO OPEN THE CLASSROOM
---------------------
  Mac:      double-click  ClassOS
  Windows:  double-click  ClassOS.bat

That's it. A small black window opens (leave it alone) and the classroom
opens in your browser.

To close down at the end of the day, close that little black window.

TO PUT IT ON YOUR DESKTOP
-------------------------
  Mac:      drag ClassOS onto the Dock, or right-click it >
            Make Alias, then drag the alias to your Desktop.

  Windows:  right-click ClassOS.bat > Send to > Desktop (create
            shortcut). Then right-click the new desktop shortcut >
            Properties > Change Icon > Browse > choose ClassOS.ico
            from this folder, so it wears the house shield.

FIRST TIME ONLY
---------------
The first launch shows a loading screen while it gathers every picture,
sound and film so nothing ever stalls in front of a class. It only
happens once. After that it opens straight away.


========================================================================
  PROTECTING YOUR TERM  —  PLEASE READ THIS PART
========================================================================

Your points, planner, quests and settings live inside your browser.
That is fast and private, but it means clearing your browsing data — or
logging into a different Windows account — can empty the board.

There are four layers protecting you. Set up the first one and you are
genuinely safe.

  1. THE BACKUP FOLDER  (do this once, today)
     Open the app > the key icon (top right) > Data & Safety >
     "Connect a backup folder" > choose the "backups" folder that sits
     right next to this file.

     From then on, every change saves itself to a real file on this
     computer, automatically. Your uploaded lesson PDFs, slides and
     songs are saved there too.

  2. A DAILY FILE IN DOWNLOADS
     Once each school day the app also drops a dated backup into your
     Downloads folder. This happens whether or not you did step 1.

  3. A COPY EVERY TIME YOU LAUNCH
     Each time you open ClassOS, it copies your backup folder into
     "backup-history" with the date and time. The last 30 launches are
     kept. Nothing in the browser can touch these.

  4. IT OFFERS ITSELF BACK
     If the app ever opens to an empty board and a backup exists, it
     says so and offers to put your term back with one button. You do
     not have to remember any of this.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
Open the app > key icon > Data & Safety > "Restore from folder".
That brings back everything: points, planner, quests, the shop,
settings, destinations, and your uploaded files.

A single .json backup file restores everything EXCEPT uploaded files
(PDFs, slides, songs) — those are too big for one file and live in the
backup FOLDER instead. So the folder is the one to rely on.


========================================================================
  IF IT WILL NOT START
========================================================================

"Python was not found" — the app needs Python, which is free:
  Windows:  https://www.python.org/downloads/
            IMPORTANT: tick "Add Python to PATH" on the first screen.
  Mac:      open Terminal and run:  xcode-select --install

Nothing else is installed, and nothing you do in the app leaves this
computer.
