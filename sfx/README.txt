Sound effects folder — Mr. D's Classroom OS
===========================================

Drop your own recordings in here, then assign them in the app:

    🗝️ Admin  →  ⚙️ Settings  →  🔊 Sound effects

Any sound you do NOT assign keeps the app's built-in one, so nothing goes
silent if a file is missing.

Format
------
Use .mp3 or .m4a. A phone voice memo works fine — AirDrop or email it to
yourself, drop it in this folder, and pick it in Admin.

Keep them SHORT. These fire during play, so anything longer than a second or
two will still be going when the next thing happens:

    Battle Day war cry   1–3 seconds   e.g. "It's Battle Day. Attack!"
    Sword clash          under 1 sec
    Blocked / heavy hit  under 1 sec
    Points awarded       under 1 sec   (this one fires a lot — keep it subtle)
    Fanfare              1–3 seconds
    Dice rattle          1–2 seconds

The war cry replaces the computer voice that reads the line today. The rest
replace short synthesised beeps.

Naming
------
Anything you like — no spaces is easiest, e.g.

    battle-cry.mp3
    swords-clash.mp3

Backups
-------
These files are NOT included in the app's backup .json (audio is too big).
Keep a copy somewhere else, the same as your videos.
