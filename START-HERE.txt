========================================================================
  MR. D'S CLASSROOM OS  —  START HERE
========================================================================

TO OPEN THE CLASSROOM
---------------------
  Mac:      double-click  ClassOS
  Windows:  double-click  ClassOS.bat

That's it. A small black window opens (leave it alone) and the classroom
opens in your browser.

To close down at the end of the day, close that little black window.

TO PUT IT ON YOUR DESKTOP
-------------------------
  Mac:      drag ClassOS onto the Dock, or right-click it >
            Make Alias, then drag the alias to your Desktop.

  Windows:  right-click ClassOS.bat > Send to > Desktop (create
            shortcut). Then right-click the new desktop shortcut >
            Properties > Change Icon > Browse > choose ClassOS.ico
            from this folder, so it wears the house shield.

FIRST TIME ONLY
---------------
The first launch shows a loading screen while it gathers every picture,
sound and film so nothing ever stalls in front of a class. It only
happens once. After that it opens straight away.


========================================================================
  PROTECTING YOUR TERM  —  PLEASE READ THIS PART
========================================================================

Your points, planner, quests and settings live inside your browser.
That is fast and private, but it means clearing your browsing data — or
logging into a different Windows account — can empty the board.

There are four layers protecting you. Set up the first one and you are
genuinely safe.

  1. THE BACKUP FOLDER  (do this once, today)
     Open the app > the key icon (top right) > Data & Safety >
     "Connect a backup folder" > choose the "backups" folder that sits
     right next to this file.

     From then on, every change saves itself to a real file on this
     computer, automatically. Your uploaded lesson PDFs, slides and
     songs are saved there too.

  1b. AN OFF-SITE COPY (worth two minutes)
     Same place > "Choose a folder" next to Off-site copy > pick the
     OneDrive or Google Drive folder that already syncs on this
     computer. Your term AND your uploaded files are copied there too,
     and your sync app carries them off this machine. There is no
     account to sign into and nothing to expire.

     The app shows you the size before it copies (Data & Safety, and
     again on the Backup & Close screen) so you always know what your
     sync app is carrying. Only files that CHANGED are copied, so the
     first run is the slow one and the rest are quick.

     IF YOU EVER RUN SHORT OF SPACE: Data & Safety has a switch,
     "Include uploaded files in backups". Leave it ON. If your cloud
     storage fills up, turning it off keeps every point, plan and
     setting backed up and leaves only the big files behind - the app
     tells you plainly that a restore will not bring those back.

  2. A DAILY FILE IN DOWNLOADS
     Once each school day the app also drops a dated backup into your
     Downloads folder. This happens whether or not you did step 1.

  3. A COPY EVERY TIME YOU LAUNCH
     Each time you open ClassOS, it copies your backup folder into
     "backup-history" with the date and time. The last 30 launches are
     kept. Nothing in the browser can touch these.

  4. IT OFFERS ITSELF BACK
     If the app ever opens to an empty board and a backup exists, it
     says so and offers to put your term back with one button. You do
     not have to remember any of this.

AT THE END OF THE DAY
---------------------
Open the app > key icon > "Backup & Close" (top left of the tab row).

It saves everything, then SHOWS you whether each copy actually saved —
green ticks for what worked, plain warnings for anything not set up or
not working, with the fix right there. Then "Close the classroom" shuts
the whole thing down, server and all.

You never have to use it: everything saves itself as you teach. It is
there so you can finish the day certain, rather than hopeful.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
Open the app > key icon > Data & Safety > "Restore from folder".
That brings back everything: points, planner, quests, the shop,
settings, destinations, and your uploaded files.

A single .json backup file restores everything EXCEPT uploaded files
(PDFs, slides, songs) — those are too big for one file and live in the
backup FOLDER instead. So the folder is the one to rely on.

WHEN CHOOSING EITHER FOLDER: make a NEW folder for it, for example
"Mr D Backups" inside Documents. The browser will not hand over the top
level of Documents, Desktop or iCloud Drive — it says only "this folder
contains system files", which looks like a fault but just means "pick a
sub-folder".


========================================================================
  SHOWING STANDINGS IN GOOGLE CLASSROOM  (optional)
========================================================================

Google Classroom cannot display a live scoreboard inside a post - it
holds text, files and LINKS, and nothing else. So the app does the next
best thing: it publishes a page, and you pin the link.

Set it up in the app > key icon > Data & Safety > "Class standings
page". You need a free GitHub access token (the panel walks you
through it, about five minutes, once).

After that:
  - Copy each class's link from that panel and post it once in that
    class's Classroom page. Pin it to the top.
  - From then on it updates itself. Award points, and the page catches
    up a minute later. It also publishes on Backup & Close.

Each class sees their own house big at the top - crest, motto, total,
and where they sit - with all four houses underneath.

WHAT LEAVES YOUR COMPUTER: house names and their points. Nothing else.
This app has never known a single student's name, so there is nothing
personal to publish.

IF YOUR SCHOOL BLOCKS THE LINK
------------------------------
Some districts block github.io outright (students use it to host
filter-bypass sites), so the page may not open for STUDENTS even if it
opens fine for you - teacher accounts are often exempt from filters.
Test it on a student device, signed in as a student, before you rely
on it.

If it is blocked you have two options:
  - Ask your tech person to allow just this one address. It is a
    single curricular page, which is usually an easy yes.
  - Use "Copy standings to paste" instead - the panel just above it in
    Data & Safety. Pick a class, click its button, and paste into a
    Classroom post. The scoreboard ends up INSIDE the post, so no
    filter can touch it. It is a snapshot rather than a live page, so
    repost it whenever you want it refreshed.

========================================================================
  IF IT WILL NOT START
========================================================================

"Python was not found" — the app needs Python, which is free:
  Windows:  https://www.python.org/downloads/
            IMPORTANT: tick "Add Python to PATH" on the first screen.
  Mac:      open Terminal and run:  xcode-select --install

Nothing else is installed, and nothing you do in the app leaves this
computer.
